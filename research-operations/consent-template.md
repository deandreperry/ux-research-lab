## Voluntary Participation

Participation is voluntary. The participant may skip a question, take a break, request an accommodation, or stop without giving a reason. State clearly whether stopping affects compensation; the default in this portfolio is full compensation for scheduled participation.

## Recording And Observation

Describe audio, video, screen, input, prototype, and observer capture separately. Recording is optional unless the study genuinely cannot proceed without it, in which case disclose that before recruitment.

## Data Use

Explain what will be collected, who can access it, where it is stored, how long it is retained, and how deletion or withdrawal works. State whether de-identified excerpts may appear in internal reports or a public portfolio. Public sharing must be optional and separately consented.

## Risks And Accommodations

Describe foreseeable privacy, emotional, physical, accessibility, employment, or technical risks and how they are reduced. Provide a direct way to request accommodations without requiring diagnosis disclosure.

- `[ ]` I consent to participate.

- `[ ]` I separately consent to possible use of a de-identified excerpt in a public portfolio.
- `[ ]` I would like a participant-facing summary when available.

Record date and consent version without committing names or signatures to a public repository.
