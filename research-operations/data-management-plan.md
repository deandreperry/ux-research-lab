## Minimization Rules

- Collect only data needed to answer approved research questions.
- Keep contact and consent data separate from study evidence.
- Remove unnecessary employer, health, account, location, and third-party details.
- Do not place recordings, raw transcripts, signatures, credentials, or participant contact information in Git.
- Review combinations of details that could indirectly identify someone.

## Access And Transfer

Name access by role, not “the team.” Use approved encrypted systems, time-limited links, and the minimum permissions necessary. Document external vendors and cross-border processing where applicable.

## Incident And Withdrawal Process

Record how the team will respond to accidental sensitive disclosure, lost access, unauthorized sharing, and participant withdrawal. Identify who owns notification, containment, deletion, and documentation.

## Public Portfolio Review

Before publication, confirm optional sharing consent, de-identification, organizational permission, intellectual-property rights, accessibility, and whether the surrounding context could still identify a participant or client.
