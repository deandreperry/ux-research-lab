## Review Cadence

- Update after the findings decision meeting.
- Revisit when the related product change ships or is declined.
- Record baseline and follow-up evidence when available.
- Do not claim sole research causation when design, engineering, market, or operational factors contributed.
- Retain declined recommendations and the rationale; influence includes clarifying why not to act.
