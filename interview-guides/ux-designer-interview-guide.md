# UX Designer Interview Guide

- **Status:** Study-ready portfolio simulation
- **Session length:** 45 minutes
- **Research questions:** FI-RQ1 current workflow, FI-RQ2 trust, FI-RQ3 role needs, FI-RQ5 accessibility

## Before The Session

- Confirm recent pattern-selection experience.
- Share duration, incentive, recording request, privacy notice, and prototype requirements.
- Ask about accessibility or communication accommodations.
- Prepare research-question IDs and structured notes.

## Interview Objective

Understand how UX designers evaluate frontend patterns, collaborate with engineers, and use research evidence when making interface decisions.

## Participant Profile

UX, product, or interaction designers who create flows, component specs, prototypes, or design system guidance for digital products.

## Opening Script

Thank you for joining. This session explores design workflow and frontend decision-making. There are no right or wrong answers. Please avoid sharing confidential employer or client information. You may skip any question, take a break, or stop at any time and still receive the stated incentive.

Before recording, confirm participation consent, separate recording consent, data use, and whether a de-identified excerpt may be considered for the public portfolio.

## Warm-Up Questions

- Tell me about your current design role and the kinds of interfaces you work on.
- What tools do you use most often during design exploration?
- How do you usually decide whether a UI pattern is appropriate?

## Core Questions

- Walk me through a recent moment when a design pattern was difficult to choose.
- What information do you expect from a component library or design system?
- How do accessibility requirements show up in your design process?
- When do you involve engineering in pattern decisions?
- What makes documentation feel trustworthy or unhelpful?
- How do research findings influence your design recommendations?

## Task-Based Prompts

- Imagine you need to choose a search/filter pattern for a content-heavy page. What would you look for first?
- Review a sample pattern entry. What is clear, missing, or unnecessary?
- Show how you would explain this pattern choice to an engineer or product manager.

## Follow-Up Probes

- What would make you more confident in that decision?
- How would this change under a tight deadline?
- What would you need to know about users before finalizing the pattern?
- What accessibility risks would you want to validate?

## Closing Questions

- If you could improve one thing about frontend pattern documentation, what would it be?
- What research artifact would be most useful to you during design work?
- Is there anything important about your workflow that I did not ask?

## Moderator Notes

- Ask for recent behavior before hypothetical preference.
- Rotate task examples when order effects could matter.
- Do not praise an answer or defend the concept.
- Record observation, participant explanation, and researcher interpretation separately.
- Note contradictions, skipped questions, and moderator assistance.
