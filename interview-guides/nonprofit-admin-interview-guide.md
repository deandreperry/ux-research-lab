# Nonprofit Admin Interview Guide

**Artifact type:** Portfolio Simulation

## Interview Objective

Understand how nonprofit staff manage website content, communicate impact, support donors, and evaluate whether a website is helping the organization meet its goals.

## Participant Profile

Nonprofit staff, communications managers, volunteer coordinators, operations leads, or administrators who contribute to website content, donor communications, or program updates.

## Opening Script

Thank you for speaking with me. This conversation is about how nonprofit teams use their websites to communicate mission, build trust, and support action. I am not evaluating your organization. I am learning about common workflows and challenges.

## Warm-Up Questions

- Tell me about your role and how you interact with the website.
- What are the most important actions visitors should take on the site?
- How often does website content need to change?

## Core Questions

- What information do donors ask for before giving?
- Which pages or workflows create the most support questions?
- How do you communicate impact without overwhelming visitors?
- What makes website content difficult to maintain?
- How do you know whether the site is working well?
- What accessibility or language considerations matter for your audience?

## Task-Based Prompts

- Imagine a first-time donor visits the site. What should they understand in the first minute?
- Walk through how you would update a program page or campaign message.
- Review a donation page. What would make it feel trustworthy or confusing?

## Follow-Up Probes

- What happens when that content becomes outdated?
- Who needs to approve this change?
- What would reduce manual work for your team?
- What information do you wish visitors understood sooner?

## Closing Questions

- If you could improve one website workflow this quarter, what would it be?
- What kind of research would help you make better website decisions?
- Is there anything important about nonprofit operations that designers often miss?
