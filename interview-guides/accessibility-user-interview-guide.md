# Accessibility User Interview Guide

**Artifact type:** Portfolio Simulation

## Interview Objective

Understand how users with accessibility needs evaluate digital learning tools, audit workflows, content clarity, and trust in accessibility guidance.

## Participant Profile

Users who rely on assistive technology, keyboard navigation, captions, high contrast settings, zoom, reduced motion, plain language, or other accessibility supports.

## Opening Script

Thank you for participating. This session is about understanding accessibility experiences and barriers. You can skip any question, pause at any time, or describe your experience in whatever way is comfortable. I am not testing you; I am evaluating how products can better support people.

## Warm-Up Questions

- Tell me about the devices, browsers, or assistive tools you typically use.
- What makes a website or learning tool feel usable to you?
- What kinds of barriers do you encounter most often?

## Core Questions

- How do you decide whether a digital product is worth continuing to use?
- What makes accessibility guidance clear or unclear?
- How do labels, headings, focus order, or error messages affect your experience?
- What accessibility issues are most disruptive during learning tasks?
- How do you prefer complex instructions to be structured?
- What makes a product feel respectful of your time and needs?

## Task-Based Prompts

- Navigate a sample audit checklist and describe what feels clear or difficult.
- Review a remediation explanation. What would help you understand the next step?
- Complete a short learning task and note any barriers, confusion, or fatigue.

## Follow-Up Probes

- What did you expect to happen there?
- How would you describe the impact of that issue?
- Is this a blocker, a slowdown, or an annoyance?
- What would make this experience more predictable?

## Closing Questions

- What should product teams stop assuming about accessibility?
- What would make this tool more useful to you?
- Is there anything I should have asked differently?
