# Moderation Script

**Artifact type:** Portfolio Simulation

## Introduction

Thanks for joining this usability session. Today we are looking at an interactive frontend learning platform. I am testing the product, not you. If anything feels confusing, that is useful feedback.

Please think aloud as you work. Tell me what you expect to happen, what you notice, and where you feel unsure. You can pause or stop at any time.

## Consent And Context

- This is a simulated portfolio research artifact.
- In a real study, participation, recording, privacy, and data handling would be confirmed before starting.
- The session would take about 30 to 45 minutes.

## Warm-Up

- What is your experience level with frontend development?
- Have you used interactive coding lessons before?
- What usually makes a learning tool helpful or frustrating?

## Task 1: Find A Lesson

Please find a lesson that would help you learn accessible form validation.

Prompts:

- What are you looking at first?
- What makes this result feel relevant or not relevant?
- What would you expect to see before starting?

## Task 2: Start The Lesson

Open the lesson and tell me what you think you will learn.

Prompts:

- Is the objective clear?
- Do the prerequisites feel clear?
- What would you do next?

## Task 3: Complete The First Coding Step

Complete the first task in the lesson.

Prompts:

- What are you trying to change?
- Is the feedback helpful?
- What would you check if the task failed?

## Task 4: Recover From An Error

Use the feedback to correct the issue.

Prompts:

- What does the error message mean to you?
- What is your next action?
- How confident do you feel right now?

## Task 5: Review Progress

Find where you can see your progress and what lesson should come next.

Prompts:

- Does the completion state feel clear?
- Do you trust the recommendation?
- What information would help you decide?

## Closing

- What was the most confusing part of the experience?
- What helped you keep going?
- What would make you more likely to return?
- Is there anything else you want the product team to know?
