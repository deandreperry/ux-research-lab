# Nonprofit Website Research Plan

- **Status:** Research proposal; organizational context and results pending

- **Decision owner:** Nonprofit leadership with communications, fundraising, programs, and web partners

## Background

This research plan supports a nonprofit website redesign focused on donation conversion, program clarity, volunteer interest, and trust-building. The site must serve donors, community members, staff, and partner organizations.

## Problem Statement

Nonprofit websites often need to communicate urgency, credibility, impact, and action paths with limited content resources. When navigation, donation flows, or program explanations are unclear, users may leave without understanding how to help.

## Research Goals

- Understand what information donors need before contributing.
- Identify barriers in donation and volunteer workflows.
- Learn how nonprofit admins manage content and measure success.
- Recommend UX improvements that increase trust and reduce friction.

## Research Questions

- What makes a nonprofit feel credible to a first-time visitor?
- Which content helps donors understand impact quickly?
- Where do users hesitate in the donation journey?
- What CMS or maintenance constraints affect the site experience?

## Target Users

- First-time donors
- Returning donors
- Volunteers
- Nonprofit staff and admins
- Community partners

## Methodology

| Method | Proposed Sample Or Scope | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Staff interviews | 4–6 people across communications, fundraising, programs, and administration | Understand content operations, approval, recurring questions, and constraints |
| Content and analytics audit | Priority mission, program, donation, volunteer, and contact pages | Establish current content, accessibility, and behavioral baselines |
| Intent-specific usability sessions | 5–6 first-time donors and 5–6 prospective volunteers | Evaluate distinct comprehension, trust, and task barriers without combining audiences |
| Post-task survey | Usability participants | Capture confidence and perceived clarity alongside observed behavior |
| Controlled experiment, if justified | Defined after baseline traffic and power analysis | Measure one validated content change only when traffic supports a responsible test |

## Product Validation Criteria

- Users can identify the nonprofit mission within 30 seconds.
- Users can find donation and volunteer paths without confusion.
- Donation flow abandonment points are documented.
- Admin needs are translated into maintainable content recommendations.

Add baselines, task definitions, denominators, and maintenance-effort measures before reporting results. Donation completion is not the only measure of a responsible experience.

## Recruitment And Sampling

Recruit donors and volunteers by recent relevant behavior and analyze them separately. Include mobile and assistive-technology use where relevant. Avoid over-recruiting existing supporters, whose familiarity may hide first-visit comprehension problems. Staff participation should cover both content decision-makers and people who perform routine updates.

## Ethics, Privacy, And Data Handling

- Do not collect donation amount, income, beneficiary status, or other sensitive information unless essential and consented.
- Avoid questions or copy that use guilt, pressure, or exaggerated impact.
- Protect the identities of staff, donors, volunteers, and program participants.
- Obtain separate recording and public-quotation permission.
- Ensure prototypes and sessions are accessible and compensate external participants.

## Analysis Plan

Analyze mission comprehension, trust reasoning, navigation, form behavior, accessibility barriers, and staff maintenance separately before connecting them. Use analytics to identify behavior patterns, qualitative research to investigate why, and content audit evidence to assess operational feasibility. Record contradictions and do not treat self-reported intent as completed behavior.

## Communication Plan

Lead the readout with prioritized decisions and operational implications. Each recommendation should name an owner, content dependency, user and organizational impact, effort, and success signal. Record leadership disposition and provide a plain-language implementation brief.

## Risks And Assumptions

- Donation behavior can be affected by income, timing, cause affinity, and trust.
- A short usability study cannot predict long-term fundraising outcomes.
- Admin workflow constraints may limit design recommendations.
- The simulation does not claim access to real donor analytics.

## Expected Outputs

- Donor journey findings
- Trust signal recommendations
- Donation flow friction map
- Admin content workflow notes
- A/B test ideas for calls to action and impact messaging
