# Nonprofit Website Research Plan

**Artifact type:** Portfolio Simulation

## Background

This research plan supports a nonprofit website redesign focused on donation conversion, program clarity, volunteer interest, and trust-building. The site must serve donors, community members, staff, and partner organizations.

## Problem Statement

Nonprofit websites often need to communicate urgency, credibility, impact, and action paths with limited content resources. When navigation, donation flows, or program explanations are unclear, users may leave without understanding how to help.

## Research Goals

- Understand what information donors need before contributing.
- Identify barriers in donation and volunteer workflows.
- Learn how nonprofit admins manage content and measure success.
- Recommend UX improvements that increase trust and reduce friction.

## Research Questions

- What makes a nonprofit feel credible to a first-time visitor?
- Which content helps donors understand impact quickly?
- Where do users hesitate in the donation journey?
- What CMS or maintenance constraints affect the site experience?

## Target Users

- First-time donors
- Returning donors
- Volunteers
- Nonprofit staff and admins
- Community partners

## Methodology

- Stakeholder interviews with nonprofit admins
- Donor journey review and heuristic evaluation
- Survey of website clarity and trust signals
- Task-based usability testing of donation and volunteer flows
- Content audit of key pages

## Success Metrics

- Users can identify the nonprofit mission within 30 seconds.
- Users can find donation and volunteer paths without confusion.
- Donation flow abandonment points are documented.
- Admin needs are translated into maintainable content recommendations.

## Timeline

| Week | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Audit current site and define priority user journeys |
| 2 | Conduct admin interviews and donor feedback survey |
| 3 | Run usability test on donation and volunteer flows |
| 4 | Deliver findings, content recommendations, and experiment backlog |

## Risks And Assumptions

- Donation behavior can be affected by income, timing, cause affinity, and trust.
- A short usability study cannot predict long-term fundraising outcomes.
- Admin workflow constraints may limit design recommendations.
- The simulation does not claim access to real donor analytics.

## Expected Outputs

- Donor journey findings
- Trust signal recommendations
- Donation flow friction map
- Admin content workflow notes
- A/B test ideas for calls to action and impact messaging
