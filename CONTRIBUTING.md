# Contributing Research Artifacts

This repository is a public portfolio and research-method library. Contributions should make the work more credible, accessible, and useful without exposing participant or client information.

## Before Adding Results

- Confirm that the study was conducted and that public sharing is permitted.
- Remove names, contact information, account details, and indirect identifiers.
- Use participant IDs only when quotations cannot reasonably identify someone.
- Record the study date, sample, method, inclusion criteria, and known limitations.
- Separate direct evidence from interpretation and recommendation.
- Obtain explicit permission before publishing photographs, recordings, or verbatim material.
- Keep participant contact, incentive, consent, recording, and raw-transcript files in approved restricted systems; the repository ignore rules are only a final safeguard.

## Artifact Checklist

- The decision and intended audience are clear.
- The evidence status is visible near the top.
- The method is justified.
- Recruitment, compensation, consent, accommodations, and data handling are addressed.
- Findings include denominators or appropriate qualitative support.
- Contradictory evidence and limitations are retained.
- Recommendations name an owner, priority, rationale, and success signal.
- Links are descriptive and images include useful alternative text.

## Writing Style

Use direct, professional language. Avoid inflated claims, universal statements from small samples, and method jargon that does not help the reader evaluate the work.
