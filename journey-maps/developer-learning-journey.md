# Developer Learning Hypothesis Journey

- **Status:** Assumption map; stages and thoughts are not participant findings

The statements in the “Thoughts” column are illustrative internal dialogue, not quotations. Replace them with consented, de-identified evidence only after research.

## Scenario

A beginner frontend learner wants to understand accessible form validation and complete an interactive coding lesson.

## User Goal

Learn a practical frontend skill, recover from mistakes, and feel confident applying the pattern in a future project.

## Stages

| Stage | Actions | Thoughts | Pain Points | Opportunities | UX Recommendations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discover | Searches for a lesson and scans topics. | "Which lesson matches what I need?" | Search results may not show skill level or prerequisites. | Make topic, skill level, and outcome visible. | Add filters and outcome-focused lesson cards. |
| Evaluate | Opens lesson overview and reads requirements. | "Can I actually do this?" | Prerequisites are easy to skip. | Build confidence before starting. | Put prerequisites and estimated time near the start action. |
| Practice | Follows coding task and checks preview. | "Did I do this correctly?" | Feedback may be too delayed or abstract. | Reinforce progress as it happens. | Add step-level validation and expected output. |
| Recover | Encounters an error and reads feedback. | "What broke, and how do I fix it?" | Error messages may not explain cause or next action. | Teach through recovery. | Include likely cause, hint, and concept link. |
| Continue | Completes lesson and reviews next step. | "What should I learn next?" | Progress is clearer after completion than during work. | Turn completion into momentum. | Recommend next lesson based on current concept. |

## UX Recommendations

- Make learning outcomes visible before lesson start.
- Treat error recovery as a teaching moment.
- Use confidence-building copy without hiding technical accuracy.
- Provide progress feedback during, not only after, task completion.

## Validation Plan

Observe discovery, practice, recovery, and continuation in task-based sessions. Record evidence IDs by stage, behavior, barrier, workaround, and emotional signal. Validate continuity between stages rather than assuming one linear journey; add alternate paths for abandonment, help-seeking, and return.
