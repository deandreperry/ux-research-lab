# Nonprofit Donor Journey

**Artifact type:** Portfolio Simulation

## Scenario

A first-time visitor is considering donating to a nonprofit after seeing a campaign shared online.

## User Goal

Understand the mission, trust the organization, and complete a donation with confidence.

## Stages

| Stage | Actions | Thoughts | Pain Points | Opportunities | UX Recommendations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arrive | Opens campaign or homepage. | "What does this organization do?" | Mission may be buried or too broad. | Establish clarity quickly. | Put mission, audience, and service area in direct language. |
| Evaluate | Looks for impact, legitimacy, and donation use. | "Will my money help?" | Impact claims can feel vague. | Build trust before asking. | Add specific outcomes, financial transparency, and current stories. |
| Decide | Clicks donation CTA and selects amount. | "How much should I give?" | Amounts may lack context. | Reduce uncertainty at decision point. | Pair donation amounts with concise impact examples. |
| Complete | Enters payment and contact details. | "Is this secure and worth finishing?" | Forms can feel long on mobile. | Keep momentum. | Shorten fields, clarify security, and show progress. |
| Confirm | Receives confirmation and next step. | "What happens now?" | Confirmation may be transactional only. | Reinforce trust and relationship. | Explain receipt, impact follow-up, and optional engagement paths. |

## UX Recommendations

- Place trust signals before and inside the donation flow.
- Make donation impact concrete without overstating certainty.
- Design the form for mobile completion.
- Use confirmation as a relationship-building moment.
