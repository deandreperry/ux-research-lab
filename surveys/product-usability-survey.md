# Product Usability Survey

**Artifact type:** Portfolio Simulation

## Survey Goal

Collect structured feedback after users interact with a frontend learning platform, focusing on navigation, task clarity, perceived learning value, and confidence.

## Target Audience

Learners testing a frontend education product or interactive documentation experience.

## Screening Questions

- What is your current comfort level with frontend development?
- Have you used interactive coding lessons before?
- Which device did you use during the session?

## Multiple-Choice Questions

- Which task felt most difficult?
  - Finding the right lesson
  - Understanding instructions
  - Completing code steps
  - Recovering from an error
  - Knowing what to do next
- Which feature helped you most?
  - Step-by-step instructions
  - Visual preview
  - Hints
  - Error feedback
  - Progress tracking

## Likert Scale Questions

Rate from 1, strongly disagree, to 5, strongly agree:

- I always knew what to do next.
- The interface helped me understand my progress.
- Error feedback helped me recover.
- I would use this product to learn another frontend topic.

## Open-Ended Questions

- What part of the experience felt confusing?
- What information did you want but could not find?
- What would make the product easier to use again?

## How Results Would Be Analyzed

Scores would be compared across experience levels and task outcomes. Qualitative responses would be coded for navigation friction, content clarity, feedback usefulness, confidence, and perceived value. Findings would be paired with usability observations before prioritizing changes.
