# Nonprofit Website Feedback Survey

**Artifact type:** Portfolio Simulation

## Survey Goal

Understand how visitors evaluate a nonprofit website's clarity, trust, donation flow, volunteer information, and sense of impact.

## Target Audience

Donors, prospective volunteers, community members, program participants, and partner organizations.

## Screening Questions

- Have you donated to or volunteered with a nonprofit in the past year?
- What brought you to the website today?
- Are you familiar with this nonprofit's mission?

## Multiple-Choice Questions

- What action were you most interested in taking?
  - Donate
  - Volunteer
  - Learn about programs
  - Contact the organization
  - Read impact stories
- What information did you look for first?
  - Mission
  - Programs
  - Donation details
  - Financial transparency
  - Location or service area
  - Volunteer requirements

## Likert Scale Questions

Rate from 1, strongly disagree, to 5, strongly agree:

- The mission was easy to understand.
- The website made the organization feel credible.
- I understood how donations would be used.
- It was easy to find the next action I wanted to take.

## Open-Ended Questions

- What information was missing or hard to find?
- What made you feel more or less confident in the organization?
- What would improve the donation or volunteer experience?

## How Results Would Be Analyzed

Results would be segmented by visitor intent. Ratings would identify clarity and trust gaps, while open-ended responses would be coded into content, navigation, donation friction, credibility signals, and action clarity.
