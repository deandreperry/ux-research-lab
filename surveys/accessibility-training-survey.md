# Accessibility Training Survey

**Artifact type:** Portfolio Simulation

## Survey Goal

Assess how product team members learn accessibility concepts, apply them during product work, and identify barriers to consistent accessibility practice.

## Target Audience

Designers, developers, QA testers, product managers, content designers, and accessibility learners.

## Screening Questions

- What is your primary role?
- How often do you work on accessibility-related tasks?
- Have you used WCAG, automated testing tools, or assistive technology testing before?

## Multiple-Choice Questions

- Which accessibility topics feel least clear?
  - Keyboard navigation
  - Screen reader behavior
  - Color contrast
  - Form errors
  - Semantic HTML
  - Cognitive accessibility
- What type of training would be most useful?
  - Short examples
  - Role-specific checklists
  - Interactive audits
  - Video walkthroughs
  - Office hours or coaching

## Likert Scale Questions

Rate from 1, strongly disagree, to 5, strongly agree:

- I know how to prioritize accessibility issues.
- I can explain the user impact of common accessibility barriers.
- Current tools help me move from issue detection to remediation.
- Accessibility guidance is written in language my team understands.

## Open-Ended Questions

- What accessibility task feels hardest to do well?
- Where does accessibility usually enter your workflow?
- What would make accessibility training easier to apply on real projects?

## How Results Would Be Analyzed

Results would be grouped by role and accessibility experience. Analysis would compare confidence with actual task frequency to identify training gaps. Open responses would be coded for themes such as prioritization confusion, tool limitations, unclear ownership, and need for practical examples.
