# Nonprofit Website Hypothesis Map

- **Status:** Assumption map for research planning; not donor, volunteer, or staff findings

## Hypotheses To Validate

- Visitors look for mission, impact, and donation use before giving.
- Donation buttons are easy to find, but donation confidence depends on context.
- Volunteer information is often split across multiple pages.
- Staff need content updates to be fast and low-risk.
- Trust is strengthened by financial transparency, real stories, and current program data.
- Visitors hesitate when forms feel long or unclear.
- Mobile users need concise paths to donate, volunteer, or contact the organization.

## Grouped Themes

These themes organize planning assumptions. Staff, donor, volunteer, program-participant, and analytics evidence should be labeled and analyzed by source before being combined.

| Theme | Supporting Observations |
| --- | --- |
| Trust before action | Users want evidence of legitimacy and impact before committing. |
| Content maintenance | Admins need simple workflows for updating programs and campaigns. |
| Action clarity | Donate, volunteer, and learn paths should be distinct but connected. |
| Emotional balance | Messaging should communicate urgency without manipulation. |
| Mobile-first completion | Key flows need to be short, readable, and resilient on mobile devices. |

## User Pain Points

- Impact statements can feel vague.
- Donation flows sometimes lack reassurance at the point of decision.
- Volunteer requirements are unclear until late in the journey.
- Admin teams may not have time to maintain complex page structures.

## User Needs

- Clear mission and service area
- Specific examples of donation impact
- Transparent contact and financial information
- Maintainable content sections for staff
- Short mobile-friendly forms

## Opportunity Areas

- Add donation impact examples near calls to action.
- Create a dedicated volunteer path with requirements and time expectations.
- Use admin-friendly page templates.
- Test donation copy, button labels, and form length.

## Product Recommendations

- Prioritize mission clarity in the first viewport.
- Add trust signals before and during the donation flow.
- Simplify volunteer navigation into one clear path.
- Build a measurement plan around donation starts, form completion, and volunteer inquiries.
