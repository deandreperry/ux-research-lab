# Nonprofit Admin Persona

**Artifact type:** Portfolio Simulation

## Background

Angela manages communications and operations for a small nonprofit. She updates program pages, coordinates volunteer information, supports fundraising campaigns, and answers questions from donors and community members.

## Goals

- Communicate mission and impact clearly.
- Help donors and volunteers take action with confidence.
- Keep website content accurate without heavy technical support.
- Reduce repetitive questions from visitors.

## Needs

- Simple content update workflows
- Clear donation and volunteer paths
- Trust-building content blocks
- Analytics that show which pages and actions matter

## Pain Points

- Limited staff time for website maintenance
- Unclear ownership of content updates
- Donation pages that do not answer donor questions
- Complex layouts that are hard to update safely

## Behaviors

- Updates site content around campaigns and events.
- Reviews donor questions to identify missing information.
- Shares impact stories through newsletters and social media.
- Prioritizes urgent operational tasks over website improvements.

## Tools Used

CMS, email marketing tools, donation platform, spreadsheets, social media tools, analytics dashboards, and shared drives.

## Accessibility Considerations

Angela needs content templates that support readable headings, meaningful links, accessible images, plain language, and mobile-friendly forms.

## Product Opportunities

- Create maintainable page templates for programs and campaigns.
- Add donation impact modules that are easy to update.
- Simplify volunteer information into a single action path.
