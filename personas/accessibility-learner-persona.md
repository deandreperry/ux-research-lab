# Accessibility Learner Persona

**Artifact type:** Portfolio Simulation

## Background

Sam is a product team member building stronger accessibility skills. They may be a designer, developer, QA tester, or content contributor who wants to apply accessibility guidance with more confidence.

## Goals

- Understand accessibility concepts beyond checklist compliance.
- Learn how issues affect real users.
- Know how to prioritize and fix common barriers.
- Build accessibility into everyday product work.

## Needs

- Plain-language explanations
- Role-specific examples
- Practical testing steps
- Clear severity and remediation guidance
- Examples that connect rules to user impact

## Pain Points

- WCAG language can feel abstract.
- Automated tools do not explain everything.
- Teams may disagree on priority or ownership.
- It is hard to know when a fix is good enough.

## Behaviors

- Looks up examples while working on real tasks.
- Relies on checklists but wants deeper understanding.
- Asks specialists for review when issues feel complex.
- Learns best from concrete before-and-after examples.

## Tools Used

Accessibility checkers, browser dev tools, screen reader references, design system docs, WCAG resources, checklists, and internal issue trackers.

## Accessibility Considerations

Sam may also need accessible training materials: clear headings, captions, keyboard-friendly examples, readable contrast, reduced motion, and plain language.

## Product Opportunities

- Provide role-based accessibility learning paths.
- Pair automated findings with human impact explanations.
- Include guided audits that teach while users work.
