## Initial Codebook

| Code | Definition | Include | Exclude |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `search-entry` | Where a participant begins a pattern search | Goal, component name, platform, accessibility need | General browsing unrelated to selection |
| `trust-signal` | Information used to judge credibility or reuse readiness | Maintenance, examples, rationale, standards | Visual preference without a trust judgment |
| `comparison` | Evidence of evaluating alternatives | Tradeoffs, rejecting one option, side-by-side needs | Looking at only one option |
| `implementation-risk` | Concern about building or maintaining a pattern | States, APIs, browser behavior, performance | General dislike without implementation impact |
| `accessibility-decision` | Accessibility information changes or confirms a choice | Keyboard, semantics, screen reader, motion | Accessibility mentioned without decision relevance |
| `handoff` | Information used to align roles | Acceptance criteria, rationale, implementation notes | Individual exploration without collaboration |

The codebook should change when the data requires it. Record additions, merges, and definition changes in an analysis memo.

## Finding Standard

A portfolio finding should include:

1. The product decision it informs.
2. The observed behavioral pattern.
3. Relevant denominators or participant coverage.
4. A de-identified excerpt or artifact reference when consent permits.
5. Counterevidence or variation.
6. The researcher’s interpretation.
7. Confidence and limitations.
8. A recommendation with owner and validation signal.

## Finding Template

### `[Finding title expressed as a user or product tension]`

- **Decision informed:** `[Decision]`

- **Variation:** `[Who behaved differently or contradictory evidence]`
- **Interpretation:** `[What the evidence may mean]`
- **Recommendation:** `[Action, owner, and priority]`
- **Confidence:** `[Low/medium/high with rationale]`
- **Validate next:** `[Remaining uncertainty]`

## Quality Review

- Can another researcher trace every finding to evidence IDs?
- Are observations and interpretations visibly separate?
- Were surprising and contradictory data preserved?
- Does participant counting avoid implying statistical prevalence?
- Could any excerpt identify a participant indirectly?
- Did the researcher search for evidence that challenges preferred solutions?
