# Frontend Index Recruitment Screener

- **Status:** Study-ready recruitment instrument
- **Target sample:** 6 discovery participants; 3 design and 3 engineering practitioners

## Participant Information

This study concerns how people select and evaluate reusable interface patterns. Screening responses are used only to determine study eligibility. Contact details should be collected in a restricted recruiting system, not committed to this repository.

## Screener Questions

1. Which role best describes your current work?
   - UX, product, or interaction designer
   - Frontend, UI, or design-system engineer
   - Product manager
   - Researcher
   - Student or learner
   - Another role: `[response]`
   - Prefer not to answer

2. During the past three months, how often have you selected, specified, reviewed, or implemented a reusable UI pattern?
   - Never — end survey
   - Once
   - Two or three times
   - Four or more times

3. Describe the most recent pattern decision you worked on. What were you trying to decide? `[open response]`

4. Which activities were part of that decision? Select all that apply.
   - Searched documentation or examples
   - Compared multiple patterns
   - Reviewed accessibility requirements
   - Discussed feasibility with design or engineering
   - Implemented or tested the pattern
   - Another activity: `[response]`

5. How many years have you worked in your current discipline?
   - Less than 1 year
   - 1–3 years
   - 4–7 years
   - 8 or more years
   - Prefer not to answer

6. Do you have a professional or personal relationship with the researcher that could make participation feel obligatory?
   - No
   - Yes — review before recruitment
   - Prefer not to answer

7. What would help you participate comfortably? Select any that apply.
   - Live captions
   - Screen-reader-compatible materials
   - Keyboard-accessible prototype
   - Questions supplied in advance
   - Additional breaks or a shorter session
   - Another accommodation: `[response]`
   - No accommodation requested

8. Are you comfortable discussing your workflow without sharing confidential employer, client, or customer information?
   - Yes
   - No — end survey

## Selection Matrix

Select participants who have recent relevant behavior and collectively provide variation in role, experience, accessibility practice, and organizational context. Do not select only highly enthusiastic design-system users. Record the reason for inclusion or exclusion without retaining unnecessary sensitive information.

## Recruiting Safeguards

- Share duration, activity, incentive, recording request, and data use before scheduling.
- Pay the full incentive when a participant stops early.
- Avoid recruiting direct reports or anyone who may feel pressured to participate.
- Never ask participants to disclose an employer’s confidential information.
